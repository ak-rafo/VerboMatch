# Verbo Match · Content audit (v27)

Scope: every verb (200 × 2 languages, all tenses generated by the app), vocab (22 topics per language), body parts, slang, phrases, idioms, faith phrases, texting list, grammar quiz questions (182 PT / 153 ES), 23 stories per language with their questions, and the sensitive-words lists.

## Conjugations
| Issue | Fix |
|---|---|
| PT past participle of **-guir** verbs was wrong: *conseguído, seguído* | Now **conseguido, seguido** (rule no longer adds an accent after silent *u*) |
| ES preterite of **reír** used the pre-2010 spelling *rió* | Now **rio** (RAE 2010). *Sonrió* keeps its accent |
| English for **faltar** said "he lacks" (wrong meaning) | Now **"he is missing / was missing"** in both languages |
| All other forms (present, preterite, imperfect, future, conditional, subjunctives, participles, gerunds, commands) | Checked, correct, including stem changes, spelling changes and irregulars |
